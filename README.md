## How I work

**Most growth problems aren't growth problems.** When acquisition is broken, the instinct is to add channels. Usually the real issue is that the thing being sold isn't clear yet. I check that first.

**If it dies when I leave, I built the wrong thing.** Deliverables expire. Systems compound. Every engagement should end with your team owning something that keeps running without me in the room.

**The hardest part is choosing what not to do.** Most teams aren't short on ideas. They're running six things at forty percent.
